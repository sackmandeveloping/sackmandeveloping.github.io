Football Quiz: Ultimate Test - Account and Data Deletion

We value our users' privacy. You can follow the steps below to request the deletion of your account and all associated data from our application.

How to Request Deletion?

To request the deletion of your account, please follow these steps:

  • Via Email: Send an email from your registered email address to [sackmandeveloping@gmail.com] with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  • Required Information: In your email, please include your in-game user ID and the Google Email Address you used to sign in.

Data That Will Be Deleted

Once your request is processed, the following data will be permanently deleted from our systems:

  • User profile and account information
  • Leaderboard rankings and scores
  • Cloud save game progress and statistics

Data Retention Period

After receiving your deletion request, your data will be completely wiped from our servers within 14 business days. No data is retained beyond this period, except where required by legal obligations.
